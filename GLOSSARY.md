# Pokémon Tournament

Pokémon drawn from the original 151 compete in a round-robin, and their results are reported as standings.

## Language

**Tournament**:
A single competition between 16 distinct Pokémon in which every Pokémon battles every other Pokémon exactly once, for 120 Battles in total.
_Avoid_: League, competition, game

**Contender**:
One of the 16 Pokémon taking part in a Tournament.
_Avoid_: Player, participant, entrant

**Battle**:
A single meeting between two Contenders that ends in a win for one and a loss for the other, or a tie for both.
_Avoid_: Match, fight, game

**Round-robin**:
The format in which each Contender meets every other Contender exactly once, giving each one 15 Battles.
_Avoid_: All-play-all, league format

**Record**:
A Contender's count of wins, losses and ties across a Tournament; the three always add up to 15.
_Avoid_: Stats, score, statistics (for a single Pokémon)
